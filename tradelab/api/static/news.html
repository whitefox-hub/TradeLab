Rate event

Direction carries the sign, so there is no negative number to type. What gets stored is sigma of the asset's pre-event volatility — the scale is a control, not a unit. §11.3 normalises every measured reaction to sigma, so a rating in sigma can be checked against what actually happened and your calibration becomes knowable. "8 out of 10 bullish" cannot be checked against anything, and an input nobody can score is the one nobody ever corrects.

Will store pick a strength

Recorded as a judgement, available from now. It cannot be backdated — a judgement dated before it was written is hindsight with a form around it, and the database refuses it.

Something the collectors did not catch, or cannot: a hack, a regulation, a war.

Fills the type, shape and a suggested rating. The suggestion is a starting point, not a verdict: a rating left exactly where the catalogue put it is recorded as template-derived, so a later calibration can tell it from one you chose. A curve built from unmoved suggestions measures the catalogue rather than you.

Recorded, but read what it is for. Sign is an input to the expected-reaction model, not a signal. What is tradeable is the residual: news that should have moved two sigma and moved a fifth of one is bearish however bullish it read, and lukewarm news that moved four is bullish. A system trading sign directly trades the part everyone has already priced.

Shape decides which machinery applies later, so it is chosen here rather than inferred. A shock decays; a schedule ends discontinuously when its date arrives; a process ends when it is decided or withdrawn.

A war, a venue collapsing, an outright ban is a regime shift, not a long shock. Every other shape estimates a deviation from a baseline and a return to it; these replace the baseline, so fitting a decay to one estimates a reversion that never happens. They never expire on a clock, and they belong to the regime classifier as a conditioning variable rather than to the reaction engine as a signal — the sample is a handful, and a handful estimates nothing.

Backdating the event is legitimate — it happened when it happened. Backdating a rating of it is not, and the database refuses that separately.

Put it back among those that influence?

Recorded as a correction with your name and the time, over the assumed horizon — not by rewinding a column. The sequence stays readable: ended, then reopened, by whom.

Mark as no longer information?

This records supersession: the item did not decay, it stopped being true. A rumour that is denied goes to zero at the denial rather than fading from it, and no clock can know that — which is why it is a button and not a timer.

It moves to No longer immediately and there is no undo on this screen. The row stays in the database with the time it was marked, so the record survives the mistake even though the screen cannot reverse it.
