Rate event

Signed, and in sigma rather than percent: 2% on BTC and 2% on a small cap are different events, 2 sigma is the same event. This is the unit the measured reaction is normalised to, so the rating can be scored against what happened.

Recorded as a judgement, available from now. It cannot be backdated — a judgement dated before it was written is hindsight with a form around it, and the database refuses it.
