Parameters

The BTC ladder's settings. Saving a change ends the current run and starts a new one under the new numbers, so a run is only ever under one configuration.

$
The money the ladder starts with, USDT. Realised profit adds to it; each part is at least $50, which the risk limits require.
How many parts the budget is split into. Each part is the budget divided by this number, and grows with realised profit.
%
A new part is bought when BTC is this far below the cheapest part held.
%
A new part is bought when BTC falls this much from its high since the last buy or the last full exit, if it is a step away from every part held.
%
Once a part reaches its minimum gain, it sells when BTC falls back this much from its highest price since.

Minimum gain by days held

Fixed in the strategy. A part never sells below its minimum; its cap is twice the minimum, at least +10%.
