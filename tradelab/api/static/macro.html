Macro

Where the money could be going instead. Rates, the dollar, equities, gold, oil and credit — the places capital leaves a risk asset for, so that "is it flowing elsewhere" has an elsewhere to point at.

Nothing here is read by any code, and that is the design rather than a gap: you are the reader. What you take from these numbers goes into the market trend on Market Sentiment, which is the one channel the algorithm has for what is happening that it cannot see. This screen shows the readings and their staleness and stops there — which way a rising dollar or a widening credit spread cuts is a market theory, and one this project has not tested. PREREG-005 is the study that would say whether the reading you form here is worth anything.
